Our Story

T&T CPA Limited was established in 2025 with a commitment to providing professional, efficient, and reliable accounting, audit, and tax services to businesses in Hong Kong and mainland China. With decades of combined experience from our founders and team members, we have served over 100 corporate clients and earned their trust and recognition.

We believe that professional accounting services go beyond record-keeping. They provide crucial support for business decision-making. Therefore, we are dedicated to delivering in-depth financial analysis, strategic tax planning, and thoughtful business advice to help your business thrive.
