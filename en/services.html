Our Professional Services

Comprehensive accounting, audit, and tax solutions for businesses and individuals

Core Services

Our professional team provides the following three core services to meet your business needs at every stage.

📊

Statutory Audit

Under Hong Kong's Companies Ordinance, limited companies must undergo annual statutory audit by licensed CPAs. We provide professional and efficient audit services ensuring financial reports comply with Hong Kong Financial Reporting Standards (HKFRS). Our experienced audit team is familiar with HKFRS and can provide tailored audit solutions for businesses of all sizes.

🏢

Accounting & Bookkeeping

We offer comprehensive accounting and bookkeeping services for SMEs, including daily transaction recording, financial statement preparation, and management account analysis, allowing you to focus on business development. We use advanced accounting software to ensure accurate and timely accounts, and regularly provide you with financial analysis reports to help you grasp business dynamics and make informed decisions.

📋

Tax Filing & Planning

The Inland Revenue Department issues tax returns to individuals, sole proprietors, partnerships, and companies. We help clients complete tax filings on time and accurately, and provide legal tax planning advice. Our tax experts are familiar with Hong Kong tax laws and the latest tax policies, ensuring compliance while maximizing tax benefits.
